# UX research summary

## Study scope and evidence

This project brief proposes short discovery interviews with 12 engineering students and 2 lab assistants. The themes below are the research assumptions encoded in the prototype brief; they are not presented as completed interviews or measured findings. Validate them with participants before treating them as research results.

## Working user needs

- Students need a quick way to check whether shared lab components and study spaces are available before walking across campus.
- Lab assistants need a visible, consistent record of what has been borrowed and by whom.
- Students sharing notes need a simple way to make useful material discoverable by course peers.
- A booking should show its current state and the name associated with it, reducing accidental double use.

## Provisional personas

**The hands-on engineering student** needs a component for a short lab session and wants to check availability from a phone between classes.

**The focused study group** needs a quiet place to meet, and wants to see which rooms can be reserved before coordinating schedules.

**The lab assistant** wants a lightweight shared view of equipment movement that students can update without installing a separate app.

These personas are hypotheses for prototyping, not claims about a completed interview sample.

## User flow

1. Open Campus Commons and scan the available, in-use, and total resource counts.
2. Enter a name or student ID, then filter by resource type or search by name.
3. Reserve an available resource. The card immediately shows its new status and the name on the reservation.
4. Return the resource when finished; it becomes available to the community again.
5. Use **List a resource** to add a resource name and category to the shared board.

## Prototype decisions to validate

- Whether students prefer name, student ID, or an authenticated identity.
- Whether a return should be limited to the original borrower or handled by a lab assistant.
- Whether rooms and equipment need reservation time windows, reminders, or condition notes.
- Whether the category filters and search are easy to find on smaller phones.
